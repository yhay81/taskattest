# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

- empathy and kindness;
- respect for differing opinions and experiences;
- constructive feedback given and accepted gracefully;
- responsibility, apology, and learning after mistakes;
- focus on the health of the whole community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing private information without permission;
- conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards and take fair corrective
action in response to inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions not aligned with this
Code and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior confidentially through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/yhay81/taskattest/security/advisories/new)
with `Conduct` at the start of the title. Reports are reviewed promptly and
fairly, with respect for reporter privacy.

Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant community impact ladder:
correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban. Severity, repetition,
and community impact determine the response.
